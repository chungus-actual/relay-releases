# Relay 0.6.10

- Run embedded terminal sessions in Relay on Windows and macOS, with tabs, nested splits, resizable panes, and inline session names. Windows uses PowerShell through ConPTY; macOS uses your login shell through native PTYs.
- Import detected iTerm2, Terminal, or compatible Ghostty display settings on macOS, including installed Nerd Fonts, colors, and cursor preferences. Reimport, rescan, or restore Relay defaults without restarting your shells.
- Pop services and terminal workspaces into their own Relay windows, then dock them back without losing sign-ins, drafts, or running sessions.
- Float active media in a separate player. On macOS, a compact native toolbar keeps Play/Pause, Full Screen, and Dock visible when resizing, without covering the video or window controls.
- Show service audio badges for active, unmuted audio. Silent video and idle Web Audio contexts no longer leave misleading badges on macOS.
- Keep real media resumable when paused immediately after starting, while excluding notification sounds and silent previews from transport controls.
- Preserve subsequent notification playback after releasing finished short notification audio.

Existing accounts, sign-ins, and settings are preserved. Terminal shells keep running when switching services, closing the terminal view, or moving between Relay and a pop-out; unloading the account or quitting Relay ends them. Shell sessions are not restored after an application restart. Terminal accounts and layouts containing them remain local and are omitted from portable setup exports.
