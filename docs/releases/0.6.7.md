# Relay 0.6.7

- Open up to four accounts together with side-by-side, stacked, grid, and main-pane layouts. Resize, reorder, focus, close, or isolate a pane while keeping its page and drafts loaded.
- Choose layouts from the visual picker beside Activity. Hide or restore the Layout button from the sidebar context menu; pane actions follow the focused account.
- Add YouTube as a separate service with account-isolated sign-in, title-bar media controls, and playback state that clears when leaving a video.
- Enable a companion in Settings → Appearance and choose Puke the cat, Roof the dog, or Rock. Companions appear to the left of the browser controls.
- Open your companion's place for pixel-art activities, outfits, occasional gaming, and a mix of original gloomy thoughts and attributed philosophy. The room follows local dawn, day, dusk, and night.
- Keep Puke mostly asleep with subtle seated music gestures. Roof takes shorter naps and can dig; Rock remains still. Animation respects reduced motion and pauses when the app is inactive.

Existing accounts and profiles are preserved. Companions are optional and disabled by default. Normal macOS builds use WebKit; Windows uses WebView2. macOS internal build: 23.
