# Relay 0.6.2

- Optional top tabs, animated drag reordering, and edge scrolling.
- Google Keep, with isolated accounts and Google sign-in handoff.
- External links open in your default browser; Capture links keeps them in Relay when enabled.
- A folder button on every download.
- Messenger-specific unread detection and per-service Dismiss for Relay badges and activity.
- Opening a hidden service restores its shortcut. Sleep gets a small moon below the icon.
- Current media title in the title bar, plus playback detection when the browser’s audio signal is missing.
- Load/Loaded controls; fresh profiles start with every service unloaded.

Available through **Restart to update**, or the installer and portable ZIP below.
