# Getting started

Install a package from the [latest release](https://github.com/chungus-actual/relay-releases/releases/latest), then choose a service in Relay and sign in on its own website. New accounts start unloaded.

## Your workspace

- Use **Settings → Add account** for another service or a separate sign-in.
- Drag service shortcuts to rearrange them. Right-click a shortcut for its account controls.
- Open **Layout** beside Activity to show up to four services. Pick an arrangement and drag the dividers to resize. Relay remembers the workspace.
- Navigation and zoom controls apply to the focused service. Playback controls follow the playing account.
- Open **Settings → Appearance → Companion** to choose Puke, Roof, or Rock. Click the companion to visit their room.

## Notifications and downloads

Unread badges and recent activity show what Relay has detected. Dismissing an account's activity clears Relay's badge; it does not mark conversations read on the website.

Sleeping services may delay alerts. Use the account's background-awake setting when timely updates matter. Provider notification and media support can vary between Windows and macOS.

The Downloads drawer tracks current-session transfers and opens their destination. Clearing finished history keeps the downloaded files.

## Updates

Packaged builds offer updates in Relay. You can also download a newer package from the release page. See [updates and checksums](updates.md) for verification and older downloads.
